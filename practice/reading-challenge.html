Practice · Lessons 1–5

Reading Challenge

346 flip cards: words, kanji and sentences from Genki Lessons 1–5. Read the front aloud, flip, and grade yourself honestly.

How to use it

Say it before you flip. Read the whole card aloud, then say what it means. Only then tap the card. The effort of pulling the answer out of memory is what makes it stick. Recognising the answer after you flip teaches very little.

Grade it strictly. Grading is how you move on: Missed it and Got it both take you to the next card. There is no Back button on purpose, so you can’t go back and peek. Mark a card Missed it if you hesitated on a single kana, misread a particle (は is wa and へ is e when they are particles), or got the meaning only roughly. A missed card comes back four cards later, tagged Again. If it was your last card it comes back straight away. Either way, the session doesn't end until you have read every card correctly once.

Mix the lessons. Leave all five lessons switched on for normal practice. A mixed deck makes you work out which lesson a card comes from as well as what it says, and that is the skill you'll need with a real menu or sign. Use a single lesson only for a short warm-up right after studying it.

Come back to your misses. Misses are saved in this browser. Start the next session with Only my misses, then do a normal mixed deck. Getting a card right cures it, and it drops out of the miss list.

Where the cards come from

Words (230) come from the vocabulary lists on the Lesson 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 pages, which are drawn from the book. Kanji (43) are Genki words written with only the kanji from the 読み書き section of Lessons 3–5. Anything later stays in kana, just as the book does it. Sentences (73) are written for this course. They use only the words and grammar of Lessons 1–5 and do not copy the book’s dialogues or exercises, because this site is public. For the book’s own sentences, read each lesson’s 会話 and 練習 with the audio.

Each card is tagged with the latest lesson it needs, so a Lesson 3 sentence can include Lesson 1 words.

Primary source: GENKI 3rd Edition official site (The Japan Times, the book’s publisher). It has the free audio (OTO Navi app), the vocabulary self-study tools and the answer keys. Use it to hear every word on these cards. Reading aloud only helps if your pronunciation is right.
